row instead. One number, one home.

**Founder rulings that frame every row below (2026-09-17, verbatim in `docs/DECISIONS_PENDING.md`):**

tap precision; **live tracking continues** when the phone moves; **indoor shell is in scope**. Rows
written before those rulings that call manual setup "the product answer" are overridden on that
point; their measurements still stand.

## How to read every number here

- **Synthetic court truth (C1, CP1):** exact projected court geometry through a known camera. The
  only truth here that resolves sub-pixel and sub-centimetre error.
- **Court gold (`data/gold/*.court.labels.json`, 20 files, 640 wide):** human-clicked FOUR corners
  per frame. **Its non-corner keypoints are computed from those clicks** (P8 C2), so it can test
  whether a court was found, never how precisely a line was placed. Human click spread ~5.8 px@640.
- **References pool (`data/<clip>_pts.json`):** strict 16 after the 2026-09-09 provenance review.
- The discipline for all of it is in `ML_PRACTICES.md`.
