**This project owns no confirmed metric footage.** The four mounts named in the corpus are
`am_hard_utr` 1.74 m, `yt_match40` 1.64 m, `demo30` 1.38 m, `flexi_joy_p01` 1.36 m — **all
below the ~2.0–2.2 m net-occlusion crossover**, so none of their courts can be *confirmed*
from a still frame. (`sAjkpeRq4P4` at 3.33 m is above it; the wider 27–29-clip corpus has
not been enumerated by height.)

Per-clip status as of 2026-09-05:

- **`yt_match40`** — re-clicked and CONFIRMED; the wrong version survives as a `.bak`, and
  the pair is the only settled truth in the corpus. The old founder ask to re-click it is
  DONE. Unchecked: whether its two withdrawn figures were actually re-derived.
- **`am_hard_utr`** — unconfirmable in principle, but corroborated by net-tape camera height
  (−3.7%) and a net-anchor render internally consistent to 0.4 px. Pre-existing depth limit
  unchanged: measurable to only 7.5 m of 23.77 m.
- **`demo30`** — unconfirmable AND un-cross-checkable (47.9 px net span is below the tape
  instrument's resolution). **Downgrade: no world-coordinate number from `demo30` is
  citable, not just its speeds.**
- **`flexi_joy_p01`** — unconfirmable; no off-plane check run.

**Why it matters:** unconfirmable is not the same as wrong — this finding retracts no
number. But the *next* metric measurement is blocked on footage that does not exist.

**How to apply:** the top founder ask is **~15 minutes: record one 2-minute clip from above
2.5 m at a real court, and note what it took to get up there.** It is simultaneously the
first compliant metric clip and the falsifier for v1's setup story — if a phone cannot reach
2.2 m at a real court with ordinary gear, v1 cuts speed and the bounce map and ships as a
shot-and-rally product. Never quote a new speed or bounce-position figure off a
below-crossover clip without saying so. Related:
[[mount-crossover-splits-v1-outputs]], [[low-camera-measurable-depth]].
